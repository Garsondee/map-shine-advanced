Something is badly wrong and the game can't wait. Hand the map to Foundry's own renderer. Nothing is lost, and you can switch back any time.

### For everyone at the table

[[do: renderer-foundry, renderer-msa]]

Foundry draws the plain map with its own lights and fog. Your Map Shine settings are kept for later.

### For this computer only

The Remote's **⋯** menu has **Stop Map Shine until reload**. It shuts Map Shine down on this computer only. Reloading with **F5** brings it back.

### Afterwards

When there's time, check [It's slow or stuttering](page:fix-slow), or report the problem from the **⋯** menu's bug link. Tell us the scene, what you did just before, and what you saw.
