### Rules for every mask

- Same folder as the map, same name plus the ending. [Naming rules](page:learn-naming).
- Same shape as the map. Any size.
- **Greyscale masks read only the red channel.** Paint in grey, or pure white and black.
- **Transparent means "not painted here"**, never black. Paint black where you mean "no".
- Very faint paint counts as nothing. Paint clearly.
