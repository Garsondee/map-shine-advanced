Two special shine masks for magic and crystal. Both effects are **off until you switch them on** in the Studio, so a map that has these masks stays calm until you want the sparkle.

### `_Iridescence`: oil-slick shimmer

A rainbow sheen that shifts as the camera moves, like oil on water, a beetle's shell or enchanted metal.

![_Iridescence mask](goblin-iridescence-mask.webp "The `_Iridescence` mask of **Goblin Alchemist**: the brew in the great cauldron.")

- **Brightness** is how strong the shimmer is. Colour is ignored: Map Shine makes its own rainbow.
- Fill the surface. Soft edges blend in nicely.

### `_Prism`: rainbow glints

Light split into little rainbows, like through cut crystal, potion bottles or ice.

![_Prism mask](goblin-prism-mask.webp "The `_Prism` mask of **Goblin Alchemist**: the glass jars on the shelves, brighter where the glass is thicker.")

- **Brightness** is where and how strongly the glints show.
- **Colour** tints the glints. Paint the mask grey for a pure rainbow, or blue for icy glints.

### Switching them on

Open **Studio → Effects → Prism** or **Iridescence** and turn the effect on for the scene.

> [!tip] Use them sparingly: a few jars, one magic sword, the crystal on the altar. A whole floor of glints is tiring to look at.
